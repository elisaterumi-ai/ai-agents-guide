## Where Should You Go From Here?

An excellent next step is to select a problem domain you understand well and think through how an AI agent could help solve it.

> What would be its core objective? What information sources would it need to query? Which tools would it invoke? At which critical moments would human sign-off be indispensable?

These guiding questions turn theoretical principles into concrete architectural decisions. Start with a tightly scoped task and a minimalist design. As you measure and evaluate real results, identifying where adjustments or additional components are needed becomes straightforward.

When experimenting, monitor both the final output and the procedural trajectory.

Observe whether the agent used tools correctly, respected permissions, and handled missing information or tool errors gracefully.

It is this balance of conceptual study, hands-on experimentation, and iterative evaluation that will help you build deep, lasting expertise in AI agents.
