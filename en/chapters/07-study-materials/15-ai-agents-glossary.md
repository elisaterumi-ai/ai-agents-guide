# AI Agents Glossary

This glossary compiles concise definitions of key terms used throughout this guide. Specific terms may carry nuanced definitions depending on architectural paradigms and tools.

- **Agency:** The capacity to act and make decisions in pursuit of a defined objective.
- **AI Agent:** A system that interprets goals, selects actions, and interacts with an environment to achieve an outcome.
- **Autonomy:** The degree of independence a system possesses to execute tasks without continuous human intervention.
- **Context:** The aggregate information made available to a model for a specific execution step.
- **Framework:** A collection of components, libraries, and abstractions that simplifies application development.
- **Guardrails:** Technical validation and safety mechanisms designed to constrain system behavior and filter actions.
- **Human Oversight (Human-in-the-Loop):** The active involvement of human operators to review, approve, or steer system actions.
- **Knowledge Base:** An organized repository of reference documents and data queried by the system.
- **LLM (Large Language Model):** A deep learning neural network trained at scale to process, understand, and generate natural language.
- **Memory:** Mechanisms to preserve relevant state and information within a single task session or across multiple sessions.
- **Multi-Agent System (MAS):** An architecture that distributes tasks, responsibilities, or domain domains across multiple collaborating agents.
- **Observability:** The capability to understand internal system state and behavior through traces, telemetry, and execution logs.
- **Orchestration:** Coordinating execution sequences, model calls, tool bindings, and termination logic.
- **Proactivity:** The capacity to anticipate requirements and plan steps ahead toward a goal.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation):** An architecture that retrieves external knowledge passages and injects them into model context to ground outputs.
- **ReAct:** A reasoning pattern that iteratively interleaves reasoning thoughts, environmental actions, and resulting observations.
- **Reactivity:** The ability to perceive and respond to environmental stimuli and changes.
- **Self-Governance:** Regulating internal behavior strictly according to pre-established policies and boundaries.
- **Stopping Condition:** Explicit programmatic logic dictating when execution must conclude.
- **System Prompt:** Foundational instructions that establish the role, persona, goals, and constraints of a language model.
- **Token:** The atomic unit of text representation used by a language model; typically corresponds to subwords or characters rather than full words.
- **Tool:** A structured API interface enabling an agent to perform external actions on third-party systems.
